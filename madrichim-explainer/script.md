# Narration and scene table (animatic v2)

Hebrew narration is the subtitle text. English is a gloss for the team only. Times are in seconds from the start of `animatic-v2.html`.

| # | Time | Hebrew narration | English gloss | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 9 | יש אנשים שהעולם עובר לידם ולא מבחין בהם. | Some people the world walks past without noticing. | Crowd of blue people walks past a line of grey people. The blue people gather into a diagonal line. |
| 2 | 9 to 17 | אבל יש מי שעוצר ורואה אותם: המדריכים והמדריכות. | But some people stop and see them: the madrichim. | The blue line shrinks into the key strap of a big white madrich icon. One grey person turns yellow. |
| 3 | 17 to 26 | בהוסטל גרים עשרים וארבעה חברים, בשלושה בתים. כל אחד שונה, וצרכיו שונים. | The hostel is home to twenty-four friends, in three houses. Each one different, with different needs. | One house holds 24 colored people, then splits into three houses with 8 each. |
| 4 | 26 to 34 | ובכל בית, בכל משמרת, שלושה מדריכים ומדריכות שנמצאים שם בשבילם. | And in every house, on every shift, three madrichim are there for them. | Zoom into one house. Three white figures with straps appear among the friends. |
| 5 | 34 to 42 | הם מגיעים כשהחברים חוזרים מהמסגרת, ועוזרים להם להבין איך ייראה המשך היום. | They arrive when the friends return from their day programs, and help them understand how the rest of the day will look. | Yellow background. A sun becomes a clock. Pairs of madrich and friend walk in. |
| 6 | 42 to 51 | אוכל, מים, מקלחת, כביסה. דברים פשוטים, אבל כל אחד מהם אומר לחבר: אכפת לי ממך. | Food, water, a shower, laundry. Simple things, but each one tells a friend: I care about you. | Food, water, shower and shirt icons become hearts, then one big heart between a madrich and a friend. |
| 7 | 51 to 60 | הם מיישמים את התוכניות שאנשי המקצוע בנו לכל חבר, כדי שיהיה רגוע ובטוח. | They carry out the plans the professionals built for each friend, so every friend feels calm and safe. | A page with messy lines straightens out, then becomes a calm circle. |
| 8 | 60 to 68 | בלילה, כשהכול שקט, הם ערים. בודקים כל שעתיים שהכול בסדר. | At night, when everything is quiet, they are awake. Checking every two hours that all is well. | Moon becomes an eye, which pulses twice (every two hours), then becomes a house with a lit window. |
| 9 | 68 to 78 | ובבוקר עוזרים להתכונן ליום. ובכל רגע שואלים: מה החבר יכול לעשות בעצמו? | In the morning, they help everyone get ready for the day. | 10 | 78 to 84 | וכשרואים אותו באמת, משהו משתנה. | And when someone is truly seen, something changes. | A grey person turns yellow and a heart appears on their chest. |
